Education

Trained at the age of 8 at the conservatory. Later, he returned to music under the guidance of his older brother Daniel Aviles (singer, composer). Then he attended jazz workshops in Bourges with François Jeanneau. Then he attended the CIM school in Paris with Antoine Hervé. Trained in jazz for a few years before devoting himself to composing for pop, EDM, jazz, and film music.
